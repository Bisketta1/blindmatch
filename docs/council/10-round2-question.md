# Council round 2 - three product questions for blindmatch

Context: see 00-framed-question.md and 07-verdict.md in this folder. Decisions since then (by the owner): matches expire (nudge at 48h, release after 7 days); everyone is both "matchable single" and "matchmaker" and consents to both; singles' date invite shows time, place, first name of the other single and 1-2 small "clues" from their profile (e.g. "likes sushi"), no photo; voice notes and Super Like come after the first test; first market Sweden.

QUESTION 1 - Several matchmakers for one single. A single (Jonathan) can have several friends swiping for him (me and Noah). I swipe yes on Miriam; Noah swipes no on Miriam. Should Miriam be shown to Noah at all? Who decides? Risk: a wrong swipe by one friend means Jonathan misses the love of his life. How should multiple matchmakers per single work? (e.g. any one yes counts; majority; each matchmaker's yes creates a separate candidate that the others can veto in chat; the single can rank/choose matchmakers; "lead matchmaker" per single; show Miriam to all matchmakers but swipes are independent...). Consider fairness, the 10-swipes-per-day rule, matchmaker chat, and the match logic on the other side too (Miriam also has several matchmakers).

QUESTION 2 - Double dates. Both singles' matchmakers can attend a four-person blind date. It is hard to know who wants one. How should the app detect/offer double dates without awkwardness (opt-in preference on profile, toggle per date proposal, matchmakers propose and singles choose, ...)?

QUESTION 3 - Language/market. Mostly Sweden first. Build Swedish-only first, or international (English + Swedish i18n) from day one? What does the choice change in the architecture, content (profile prompts), legal side and growth?

Answer concretely for a solo free-tier Expo + Supabase build.
