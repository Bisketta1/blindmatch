# Council round 2 verdict (5 advisors answered; peer review round skipped for speed, chairman = main agent)

Advisor picks: Contrarian, First Principles, Executor, Outsider and Expansionist all answered independently (raw answers were relayed in the session; key points below).

## Q1 Several matchmakers per single - 5/5 agree
- Swipes are independent. A "no" is a private pass, never a veto. One wrong "no" must not cost someone the love of their life.
- Any one "yes" creates a candidate (proposal) and opens a chat thread. Other matchmakers of that single see "Noah said yes" and can discuss. Objecting is a chat message, not a mechanic.
- 10 swipes/day per matchmaker-single pair. Max 3 open candidates per single (Contrarian). One thread per candidate.
- The other side works the same: a match exists when both sides have at least one yes. A second yes adds a "2 friends agree" badge (First Principles).
- The invite credits the matchmakers: "Erik and Sara thought you'd click" (Outsider).
- The single can mute a matchmaker. No lead matchmaker, no majority vote.
- Conflict: Contrarian wanted the single to see any matchmaker's yes; that breaks the blind premise. Chairman call: the single sees nothing until the invite.
- Parked (Expansionist): Panel Room group chat, Boost/Veto tokens, per-single taste profile from swipes, matchmaker trust score.

## Q2 Double dates - 5/5 agree
Never inferred from behaviour. Private profile setting "open to double date" (default off). A matchmaker can tick "+ I'll come" on a proposal only if both singles have it on; singles still accept/decline the whole date. Columns added now, feature after the first test. Later (Expansionist): "Plus-Two" invite badge, Blind Date Night, chaperone mode.

## Q3 Language - 4 of 5 agree
Swedish copy first, but every string through i18n (i18next + expo-localization, sv.json) from day one; profile prompts stored as ids with locale text, written natively in Swedish; `locale` column on profiles; Swedish privacy policy (IMY, GDPR), 18+. Expansionist wanted English from day one too and a Nordic campus-by-campus rollout: parked until a real user needs English.
